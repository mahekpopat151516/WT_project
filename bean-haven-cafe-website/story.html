Where It Began

A little café with a big love for coffee.

Bean Haven began with a simple idea: create a place where exceptional coffee feels approachable and every guest feels at home. We partner with thoughtful roasters, bake fresh each morning and keep our space warm, calm and welcoming.

Today, the same spirit guides every cup we serve.
